# Research and writing skills

## res.research-method
title: Research a question properly
roles: employee, pm, ceo, risk
stages: discovery
keywords: research, sources, investigate, study, evidence, findings, literature
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Write the exact question and what a good answer looks like. 2. Search several independent, reputable sources. 3. Prefer primary sources (official data, papers, documents). 4. Note date, author and possible bias for each. 5. Separate facts, estimates and opinions. 6. Look for evidence against your first idea. 7. Summarize with citations and a confidence level.

## res.source-evaluation
title: Judge whether a source is reliable
roles: employee, risk, qa
stages: discovery, quality
keywords: source, sources, reliable, credibility, fact, check, misinformation, citation
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Who wrote it and what do they gain? 2. Is it recent enough? 3. Does it cite data you can check? 4. Do other independent sources agree? 5. Is it opinion, news, research or advertising? 6. Watch for emotional or absolute language. 7. When unsure, say so; never present an unverified claim as fact.

## write.technical-writing
title: Clear technical writing
roles: employee, pm
stages: implementation, release
keywords: writing, technical, document, guide, manual, explain, clear, instructions
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Know the reader and what they must do after reading. 2. Lead with the answer or the result. 3. Short sentences, active voice, common words. 4. Numbered steps for procedures; one action per step. 5. Examples and expected output. 6. Headings that say what is inside. 7. Remove anything the reader does not need.

## write.summary
title: Summarize a long text
roles: employee, pm, ceo, chat
stages: discovery
keywords: summary, summarize, summarise, notes, brief, tldr, overview, key, points
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Read fully first. 2. One-sentence main point. 3. 3-5 key points in order of importance. 4. Keep numbers, names and decisions exact. 5. Mark open questions and actions with owners. 6. Do not add opinions that were not in the text. 7. Keep it under a tenth of the original length.

## write.hinglish-translation
title: Write or translate for Indian readers (Hindi, Hinglish, English)
roles: employee, chat
stages: implementation, release
keywords: hindi, hinglish, translation, translate, language, indian, localization, local
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Match the reader: simple Hinglish for casual users, clear English or Hindi for formal use. 2. Keep technical words in English when people use them that way (app, login, payment). 3. Translate meaning, not word by word. 4. Short sentences; avoid idioms that do not travel. 5. Keep numbers, dates and currency in local format (₹, DD-MM). 6. Ask a native speaker to read important text. 7. Stay polite and respectful in tone.

## write.email-professional
title: Professional email
roles: employee, ceo, pm, chat
stages: release
keywords: email, mail, message, professional, reply, follow-up, formal, letter
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Subject says the topic and any action. 2. First line: why you are writing. 3. One topic per email; short paragraphs. 4. Clear ask with a date. 5. Polite, direct, no jargon. 6. Check names, attachments and recipients before sending. 7. Sign with name and role.
