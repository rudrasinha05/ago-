# Graphics and brand skills

## gfx.logo-brief
title: Logo and brand mark
roles: employee
stages: discovery, implementation
keywords: logo, brand, mark, icon, identity, symbol, branding
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Brief: name, audience, personality (3 words), competitors to differ from. 2. Sketch many simple ideas before refining one. 3. It must work tiny (16 px), in one color and on dark and light. 4. Avoid trends, clip art and other brands' look-alikes. 5. Deliver SVG plus PNG sizes; clear space and minimum size rules. 6. Check the name is free to use (trademark search). 7. Write a one-page usage guide.

## gfx.brand-identity
title: Brand identity kit
roles: employee, ceo
stages: discovery, implementation
keywords: brand, identity, colors, typography, voice, guidelines, style
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Positioning line: for whom, what, why different. 2. Palette: primary, secondary, neutrals, status colors with contrast checked. 3. Two typefaces with a size scale. 4. Logo versions and rules. 5. Imagery style: photos or illustrations, mood. 6. Voice: 3 traits with do/don't examples. 7. Templates for social posts, slides and documents.

## gfx.illustration-prompt
title: Image or illustration from an AI image model
roles: employee
stages: implementation
keywords: image, illustration, generate, prompt, midjourney, dall-e, picture, artwork, visual
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Describe subject, setting, style, colors, lighting, framing and mood in that order. 2. Say what to avoid (text, extra fingers, logos). 3. Use original characters only; never copy a known character, brand or artist's signature style. 4. No real people without consent. 5. Generate several, pick, then refine one detail at a time. 6. Check rights and the tool's license for commercial use. 7. Keep the prompt with the final image.

## gfx.social-graphics
title: Social media graphics
roles: employee
stages: implementation
keywords: social, media, post, instagram, banner, thumbnail, graphic, poster
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Right size per platform (1080x1080 square, 1080x1920 story, 1280x720 thumbnail). 2. One message per graphic; readable in one second on a phone. 3. Big text, high contrast, few words. 4. Brand colors and fonts every time. 5. Keep key content away from edges and platform UI. 6. Alt text for every image. 7. Make a reusable template.

## gfx.presentation-design
title: Slide deck design
roles: employee, pm, ceo
stages: release, discovery
keywords: slides, presentation, deck, pitch, powerpoint, keynote, story
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Story first: problem, solution, proof, ask. 2. One idea per slide with a headline that states the point. 3. Few words; speak the details. 4. Charts simplified to the one comparison that matters. 5. Consistent template, fonts and colors. 6. High contrast, large text (24 pt+). 7. End with a clear next step.

## gfx.svg-icons
title: Icons and SVG graphics
roles: employee
stages: implementation
keywords: svg, icon, icons, vector, graphic, stroke, illustration
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Draw on a fixed grid (24x24) with consistent stroke width and corner radius. 2. Simple shapes; readable at 16 px. 3. Use currentColor so icons follow text color. 4. Optimize the SVG (remove metadata, round numbers). 5. Add aria-hidden or a title for meaning. 6. Same visual weight across the set. 7. Test in light and dark themes.

## gfx.video-thumbnail-motion
title: Short video and motion graphics
roles: employee
stages: implementation
keywords: video, motion, animation, reel, short, edit, youtube, thumbnail
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Hook in the first 2 seconds. 2. Script: one message, then call to action. 3. Vertical 9:16 for reels/shorts, 16:9 for YouTube. 4. Captions on screen (most watch muted). 5. Cut every pause; keep rhythm. 6. Licensed or original music only. 7. Thumbnail: face or object, 3-5 big words, strong contrast.
