# Sales and customer skills

## sales.outreach
title: Outreach to potential customers
roles: employee, ceo
stages: release
keywords: outreach, cold, email, prospect, lead, leads, sales, linkedin, message
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Contact only people who fit the customer profile, through allowed channels. 2. Research each one briefly. 3. Message: relevant observation, the problem you solve, proof, a small ask. 4. Under 100 words. 5. Follow up 2-3 times, politely, then stop. 6. Respect opt-outs immediately. 7. Track replies and meetings per message version.

## sales.discovery-call
title: Sales discovery call
roles: employee, ceo
stages: release
keywords: sales, call, discovery, demo, qualify, needs, budget, meeting
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Agenda and time check at the start. 2. Ask about their situation, problem, impact and current solution. 3. Qualify: budget, decision maker, need, timeline. 4. Listen more than talk (70/30). 5. Show only what solves their stated problem. 6. Agree a concrete next step and date. 7. Send a short recap the same day.

## sales.proposal
title: Write a proposal or quotation
roles: employee, finance, ceo
stages: release
keywords: proposal, quotation, quote, rfp, offer, bid, estimate, client
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Restate the client's problem in their words. 2. Proposed solution and deliverables. 3. Timeline with milestones. 4. Price, payment terms, validity, taxes. 5. What is not included. 6. Why us: relevant proof. 7. Next step to accept. One clear page summary on top.

## sales.negotiation
title: Negotiate a deal
roles: ceo, finance, employee
stages: release
keywords: negotiation, negotiate, deal, discount, terms, contract, price
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Know your walk-away point and best alternative before talking. 2. Understand their real interests. 3. Trade, do not give: every concession gets something back. 4. Negotiate the whole package (scope, time, payment), not only price. 5. Use silence and summaries. 6. Put agreements in writing quickly. 7. Keep the relationship good for the next deal.

## sales.customer-support
title: Customer support replies
roles: employee
stages: release
keywords: support, customer, ticket, complaint, help, reply, service, refund
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Reply fast, even if only to confirm receipt. 2. Thank them and restate the issue. 3. Give the fix in numbered steps, or the honest next step and time. 4. Apologize for our mistakes plainly. 5. Never blame the customer. 6. Close the loop: confirm it is solved. 7. Log the cause so the product can fix it.

## sales.customer-success
title: Keep customers successful
roles: employee, pm
stages: release
keywords: retention, churn, customer, success, onboarding, renewal, loyalty
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Define the first success moment and get every new customer there fast. 2. Onboarding checklist and a check-in in week one. 3. Watch usage signals for risk. 4. Regular value reviews with numbers. 5. Collect feedback and act on it visibly. 6. Ask happy customers for referrals and reviews (real ones only). 7. Learn from every customer who leaves.
