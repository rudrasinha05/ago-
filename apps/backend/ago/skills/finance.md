# Finance skills

## fin.budget
title: Make and track a budget
roles: finance, pm, ceo
stages: discovery, architecture
keywords: budget, spend, cost, costs, expense, allocation, money, credits
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. List every cost: people, tools, AI credits, hosting, marketing, taxes. 2. Split fixed and variable costs. 3. Set limits per category with an owner. 4. Keep a reserve (10-20%). 5. Compare actual vs budget monthly. 6. Explain every big difference. 7. Approve overruns before, not after.

## fin.unit-economics
title: Unit economics
roles: finance, ceo
stages: discovery
keywords: unit, economics, cac, ltv, margin, payback, churn, profitability
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) = sales and marketing spend / new customers. 2. Gross margin per customer after delivery costs. 3. Lifetime value (LTV) = monthly margin x average months retained. 4. Healthy target: LTV at least 3x CAC. 5. Payback period under 12 months. 6. Calculate per channel and plan. 7. Fix the weakest number first.

## fin.invoicing-gst
title: Invoices and Indian GST basics
roles: finance, employee
stages: release
keywords: invoice, invoicing, gst, tax, billing, payment, india, receipt
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Invoice: seller and buyer names, addresses, GSTIN if registered, invoice number and date, item description, SAC/HSN code, amount, tax rate and tax, total, payment terms. 2. Number invoices in one series without gaps. 3. Charge CGST+SGST within the state, IGST across states. 4. Keep copies for the legally required period. 5. Track due dates and follow up politely. 6. Registration thresholds and rates change: confirm with a CA. 7. This is general information, not tax advice.

## fin.cash-flow
title: Cash flow management
roles: finance, ceo
stages: discovery, release
keywords: cash, flow, runway, payments, receivables, payables, liquidity
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Weekly cash forecast for 13 weeks. 2. Invoice promptly; shorten payment terms; take advance payments for projects. 3. Pay suppliers on time, not early. 4. Watch runway in months. 5. Keep an emergency reserve. 6. Avoid large fixed commitments before revenue is steady. 7. Act early when runway drops below 6 months.

## fin.cost-of-ai
title: Control AI model costs
roles: finance, cto, employee
stages: architecture, implementation
keywords: ai, cost, tokens, api, credits, llm, spend, usage, model
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Use the cheapest model that passes the quality bar; local first. 2. Keep prompts short; send only needed context. 3. Cap output tokens. 4. Cache repeated results. 5. Set monthly limits per provider and in the provider dashboard. 6. Track cost per task and per project. 7. Review the top spenders monthly.
