# People and HR skills

## hr.job-description
title: Write a job or role description
roles: ceo, pm
stages: discovery
keywords: job, description, role, hiring, position, responsibilities, skills, requirements
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Role purpose in one sentence. 2. 5-7 responsibilities with outcomes. 3. Must-have skills vs nice-to-have. 4. How success is measured in the first 90 days. 5. Who they report to and work with. 6. Inclusive language; no unnecessary requirements. 7. For AI employees: allowed tools, limits and approval rules.

## hr.hiring-interview
title: Hire with structured interviews
roles: ceo, pm
stages: discovery
keywords: hiring, interview, candidate, recruit, recruitment, selection, assessment
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Decide the scorecard before seeing candidates. 2. Same questions for everyone, tied to the scorecard. 3. Ask for past examples (what did you do, result). 4. A short real work sample, paid if long. 5. Each interviewer scores independently first. 6. Check references. 7. Decide on evidence; avoid bias from looks, accent or background.

## hr.onboarding
title: Onboard a new team member
roles: pm, ceo
stages: implementation
keywords: onboarding, new, employee, joiner, training, orientation, first, week
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Access and tools ready on day one. 2. A buddy for questions. 3. First-week plan with a small real task. 4. Explain goals, rules, how decisions and approvals work. 5. 30/60/90-day goals. 6. Check in weekly for the first month. 7. Ask what was confusing and fix the onboarding.

## hr.performance-feedback
title: Give feedback and review performance
roles: ceo, pm, quality
stages: quality, release
keywords: feedback, performance, review, appraisal, coaching, improvement, evaluation
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Base it on observed work and results, not personality. 2. Situation, behavior, impact, then what to change. 3. Praise specifically and in time. 4. Agree clear goals with dates. 5. Offer support and training. 6. Document fairly. 7. For AI employees: use QA pass/fail history and receipts as the evidence.
