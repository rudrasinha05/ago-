# Education and training skills

## edu.course-design
title: Design a short course
roles: employee, pm
stages: discovery, architecture
keywords: course, training, curriculum, lesson, learning, teach, module, education
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Who the learner is and what they can do after the course (3-5 outcomes). 2. Modules ordered from basics to applied. 3. Each lesson: one idea, an example, a practice task. 4. Short videos (5-10 min) or readings. 5. Quizzes and a final project that proves the outcomes. 6. Clear time needed and prerequisites. 7. Collect feedback and update.

## edu.lesson-plan
title: Lesson plan
roles: employee
stages: implementation
keywords: lesson, plan, class, teaching, students, activity, lecture
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Objective: what students can do by the end. 2. Hook to start (question, story). 3. Explain with one clear example. 4. Guided practice, then independent practice. 5. Check understanding with quick questions. 6. Summary and homework. 7. Time for each part; adapt for slower and faster learners.

## edu.quiz-assessment
title: Quizzes and assessments
roles: employee, qa
stages: implementation, quality
keywords: quiz, test, exam, assessment, questions, mcq, grading, rubric
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Each question tests one stated outcome. 2. Clear, unambiguous wording. 3. MCQ distractors plausible but clearly wrong. 4. Mix recall, application and reasoning. 5. Rubric for open answers. 6. Explain the right answer after. 7. Review questions many students miss.
