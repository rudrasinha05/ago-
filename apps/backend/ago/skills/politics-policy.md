# Politics and public policy skills

## pol.policy-analysis
title: Analyze a public policy neutrally
roles: employee, risk, ceo, chat
stages: discovery
keywords: policy, public, government, politics, political, analysis, law, scheme, regulation
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. State the policy's goal and exact content from the official text. 2. Who gains, who pays, who is affected. 3. Evidence on similar policies elsewhere. 4. Costs, benefits and trade-offs with sources. 5. Present the strongest arguments of supporters and critics fairly. 6. Separate facts from values and predictions. 7. No personal political verdict; let the reader decide.

## pol.stakeholder-mapping
title: Map stakeholders and influence
roles: employee, ceo, pm, risk
stages: discovery
keywords: stakeholder, stakeholders, influence, power, interest, mapping, government, community
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. List everyone affected or able to affect the outcome. 2. Rate each by influence and interest. 3. Note their goals, concerns and current position, with evidence. 4. Find allies, neutrals and opponents. 5. Plan honest engagement per group. 6. Respect laws on lobbying and disclosure. 7. Update as positions change.

## pol.election-campaign-compliance
title: Political campaign work within the law
roles: employee, risk
stages: discovery, release
keywords: election, campaign, political, voters, candidate, party, manifesto, outreach
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Follow election commission rules: spending limits, disclosures, silence periods, model code of conduct. 2. Honest messages only; no fake news, deepfakes or impersonation. 3. Use voter data only as the law allows; respect privacy and opt-outs. 4. Label paid political ads. 5. No targeting by religion, caste or other protected traits to divide people. 6. Keep records of spend and content. 7. Get legal review before launch.

## pol.government-tender
title: Government tenders and public procurement
roles: employee, finance, ceo, risk
stages: discovery, release
keywords: tender, government, procurement, gem, bid, rfp, public, contract, eligibility
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Find tenders on official portals (e.g. GeM, CPPP, state portals). 2. Read eligibility, documents, deadlines and evaluation criteria first. 3. Decide go/no-go on fit and margin. 4. Prepare every required document exactly; missing papers mean rejection. 5. Price with all costs and taxes. 6. Submit before the deadline; keep receipts. 7. Never offer gifts or improper influence.

## pol.civic-communication
title: Public and civic communication
roles: employee, chat
stages: release
keywords: civic, public, communication, citizens, awareness, campaign, notice, community
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. One clear message and action for citizens. 2. Simple local language; include those with low literacy (pictures, audio). 3. Correct facts from official sources. 4. Accessible formats. 5. Neutral and respectful tone for all groups. 6. Give a contact for questions. 7. Correct mistakes openly and fast.
