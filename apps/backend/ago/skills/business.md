# Business and strategy skills

## biz.opportunity-finding
title: Find business opportunities
roles: ceo, pm, employee, finance
stages: discovery
keywords: opportunity, opportunities, market, idea, niche, demand, trend, problem, earning
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Start from problems people already pay to solve or complain about (reviews, forums, job posts, tenders). 2. Look for changes: new rules, technology, prices, behavior. 3. Size it: who has the problem, how many, what they pay today. 4. Check competition and why customers still complain. 5. Match with our strengths (AI software delivery). 6. Score: pain, willingness to pay, reach, fit, speed to first revenue. 7. Pick one to test with a small offer before building.

## biz.market-research
title: Market research
roles: ceo, pm, employee, finance
stages: discovery
keywords: market, research, size, tam, sam, som, industry, segment, trends
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Define the customer segment precisely. 2. Size top-down (reports) and bottom-up (customers x price); trust bottom-up more. 3. Note growth and drivers. 4. List competitors and substitutes. 5. Talk to real customers to check assumptions. 6. Cite every source with date. 7. Conclude: is it big enough, growing, and winnable for us?

## biz.competitor-analysis
title: Competitor analysis
roles: ceo, pm, employee
stages: discovery
keywords: competitor, competition, competitive, analysis, alternatives, comparison, rivals
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. List direct competitors, indirect ones and "do nothing". 2. For each: target customer, offer, price, strengths, weaknesses. 3. Read their customer reviews for unmet needs. 4. Compare on what customers value, not feature counts. 5. Find a gap we can own. 6. Note how they could respond. 7. Refresh every quarter.

## biz.business-model
title: Business model
roles: ceo, finance, pm
stages: discovery
keywords: business, model, revenue, canvas, monetization, subscription, value, proposition
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Customer segments and their job to be done. 2. Value proposition: why us over alternatives. 3. Channels to reach them. 4. Revenue model: one-time, subscription, usage, commission, services. 5. Key costs and resources. 6. Unit economics: customer acquisition cost vs lifetime value. 7. The riskiest assumption and how to test it cheaply.

## biz.pricing
title: Price a product or service
roles: ceo, finance, pm
stages: discovery, release
keywords: pricing, price, plans, tiers, cost, value, discount, monetize
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Know the cost floor (delivery + support + acquisition). 2. Find the value to the customer and competitors' prices. 3. Price on value, not only cost. 4. 2-3 tiers by customer type, one clear recommended tier. 5. Simple units customers understand. 6. Test prices with real offers; watch conversion. 7. Review yearly; grandfather loyal customers fairly.

## biz.swot-strategy
title: Strategy with SWOT and goals
roles: ceo, pm
stages: discovery
keywords: strategy, swot, goals, okr, vision, mission, objectives, direction
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. SWOT with evidence, not opinions. 2. Choose where to play and how to win in one sentence each. 3. 3 objectives for the period; each with 2-4 measurable key results. 4. Say what we will NOT do. 5. Assign owners. 6. Review monthly with numbers. 7. Change course openly when evidence says so.

## biz.financial-projection
title: Financial projection
roles: finance, ceo
stages: discovery
keywords: financial, projection, forecast, revenue, profit, cash, runway, budget, model
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Drivers first: customers, price, churn, conversion, costs. 2. Monthly for year one, yearly after. 3. Three cases: conservative, base, optimistic. 4. Cash flow, not only profit; show runway. 5. State every assumption and its source. 6. Sensitivity: which driver moves the result most. 7. Update with actuals monthly.

## biz.pitch
title: Pitch to investors or partners
roles: ceo, finance
stages: release, discovery
keywords: pitch, investor, investors, funding, deck, startup, partner, raise
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Problem and who has it. 2. Solution and why now. 3. Market size. 4. Traction with real numbers. 5. Business model and unit economics. 6. Team and why us. 7. The ask: amount, use of funds, milestones it reaches. Keep to 10-12 slides; practice the 2-minute version.

## biz.partnerships
title: Partnerships and deals
roles: ceo, finance, risk
stages: discovery, release
keywords: partnership, partner, deal, alliance, collaboration, reseller, agreement
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Define what each side gives and gets. 2. Check the partner's reputation and customers. 3. Start with a small pilot and clear success numbers. 4. Put scope, money, data, IP and exit terms in writing. 5. One owner on each side. 6. Review results on a set date. 7. Expand only on evidence.
