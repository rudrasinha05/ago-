# Marketing skills

## mkt.go-to-market
title: Go-to-market plan
roles: ceo, pm, employee
stages: release, discovery
keywords: go-to-market, gtm, launch, marketing, channels, customers, acquisition
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Ideal customer profile and the problem message. 2. Positioning against alternatives in one line. 3. Pick 1-2 channels where those customers already are. 4. Offer and call to action. 5. Launch plan with dates and owners. 6. Metrics: reach, sign-ups, activation, paying customers, cost per customer. 7. Weekly review; double down on what works.

## mkt.seo
title: Search engine optimization
roles: employee
stages: implementation, release
keywords: seo, search, google, keywords, ranking, content, organic, website
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Find keywords real customers search, with intent. 2. One main topic per page; title and H1 contain it naturally. 3. Answer the question better than current results. 4. Fast pages, mobile friendly, clean URLs. 5. Meta description that earns the click. 6. Internal links between related pages. 7. Track rankings and clicks in Search Console; update old content.

## mkt.content-marketing
title: Content marketing
roles: employee, pm
stages: release
keywords: content, blog, article, posts, marketing, newsletter, editorial, calendar
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Topics from customer questions and problems. 2. A content calendar with formats and owners. 3. Each piece: one idea, useful on its own, clear next step. 4. Show proof: examples, numbers, stories. 5. Repurpose one piece into posts, a thread, a short video. 6. Distribute, do not only publish. 7. Measure reads, shares and sign-ups per piece.

## mkt.social-media
title: Social media marketing
roles: employee
stages: release
keywords: social, media, instagram, linkedin, twitter, x, facebook, youtube, posts, followers
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Pick platforms where the customers are; do 1-2 well. 2. Content pillars: teach, show work, behind the scenes, proof, offer. 3. Consistent schedule. 4. Native formats per platform (reels, carousels, threads). 5. Reply to comments quickly. 6. Clear call to action in bio and posts. 7. Track saves, shares and clicks, not only likes.

## mkt.email
title: Email marketing
roles: employee
stages: release
keywords: email, newsletter, mailing, list, campaign, subscribers, automation, drip
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Only email people who opted in; easy unsubscribe always. 2. Welcome sequence that delivers value first. 3. One goal per email and one button. 4. Subject line: specific and honest. 5. Short, personal tone, mobile-friendly. 6. Segment by interest or behavior. 7. Track opens, clicks, conversions; clean inactive contacts.

## mkt.copywriting
title: Persuasive marketing copy
roles: employee
stages: release, implementation
keywords: copywriting, copy, headline, landing, page, ad, persuasive, sales, message
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Know the reader's problem and words. 2. Headline: the outcome they want. 3. Show benefits, then features as proof. 4. Specifics beat adjectives (numbers, names, examples). 5. Handle the main objection. 6. One clear call to action. 7. Honest claims only; no fake reviews, urgency or testimonials.

## mkt.ads
title: Paid advertising
roles: employee, finance
stages: release
keywords: ads, advertising, paid, campaign, google, meta, facebook, budget, cpc, roas
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Clear goal and target cost per customer before spending. 2. Start small with tight targeting. 3. 3-5 ad variations; one change at a time. 4. Landing page matches the ad's promise. 5. Track conversions properly before scaling. 6. Stop losers fast; scale winners slowly. 7. Follow platform policies and honest claims.

## mkt.landing-page
title: Landing page that converts
roles: employee, pm
stages: implementation, release
keywords: landing, page, website, conversion, signup, hero, cta, homepage
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Hero: who it is for, the outcome, one primary button. 2. Proof near the top: logos, numbers, real testimonials. 3. Benefits in 3-4 blocks with visuals. 4. How it works in 3 steps. 5. Pricing or the next step clearly. 6. FAQ for objections. 7. Fast, mobile-first, tracked; test one change at a time.
