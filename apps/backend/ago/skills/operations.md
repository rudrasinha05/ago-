# Operations skills

## ops.sop
title: Standard operating procedure (SOP)
roles: pm, employee, quality
stages: release, implementation
keywords: sop, procedure, process, checklist, operations, workflow, steps, standard
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Purpose and when to use it. 2. Owner and roles. 3. Numbered steps, one action each, in order. 4. Inputs, tools and where things are stored. 5. Checks at risky steps. 6. What to do when something goes wrong. 7. Version, date and review schedule; test it by having someone else follow it.

## ops.process-improvement
title: Improve a process
roles: pm, employee, ceo
stages: quality, release
keywords: process, improvement, efficiency, bottleneck, automation, lean, workflow
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Map the current process step by step with times. 2. Measure where work waits or is redone. 3. Find the biggest bottleneck. 4. Remove, simplify, then automate, in that order. 5. Try the change small first. 6. Measure the result against the old numbers. 7. Update the SOP and train people.

## ops.vendor-selection
title: Choose a vendor or tool
roles: pm, finance, risk, cto
stages: architecture, discovery
keywords: vendor, tool, supplier, software, selection, saas, procurement, compare
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Write the requirements and must-haves first. 2. Shortlist 3 options. 3. Compare cost (total, not only price), features, security, support, lock-in. 4. Check reviews and references. 5. Trial with real work. 6. Read terms: data, exit, price changes. 7. Record the decision and the reason.

## ops.customer-feedback-loop
title: Customer feedback loop
roles: pm, employee
stages: release, quality
keywords: feedback, survey, nps, reviews, customers, insights, voice
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Collect feedback in product, support tickets, reviews and calls. 2. Tag each item by theme and customer type. 3. Count frequency and impact. 4. Share a monthly summary with the team. 5. Decide what to act on. 6. Tell customers what changed because of them. 7. Never edit or fake reviews.
