# Legal and compliance skills

## legal.contract-review
title: Review a contract (checklist)
roles: risk, ceo, finance
stages: release, discovery
keywords: contract, agreement, terms, clause, legal, review, liability, nda
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Parties, scope and deliverables are exact. 2. Payment amount, schedule, taxes, late fees. 3. IP ownership and licenses. 4. Confidentiality and data protection. 5. Liability caps, indemnities, warranties. 6. Term, termination, notice and what happens after. 7. Governing law and dispute resolution. Flag anything unusual for a qualified lawyer; this checklist is not legal advice.

## legal.terms-privacy
title: Terms of service and privacy policy
roles: risk, employee
stages: release
keywords: terms, service, privacy, policy, tos, legal, website, app, users
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Privacy policy: what data, why, legal basis, who it is shared with, retention, user rights, contact. 2. Match the policy to what the product really does. 3. Terms: who may use it, acceptable use, payments, refunds, liability, termination. 4. Plain language summary on top. 5. Cover the laws where users are (e.g. India DPDP Act, GDPR). 6. Version and date them; notify users of changes. 7. Have a lawyer review before launch.

## legal.open-source-licenses
title: Open-source licenses
roles: risk, cto, employee
stages: architecture, release
keywords: license, licence, open, source, mit, apache, gpl, copyright, attribution
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Check the license of every dependency and copied snippet. 2. Permissive (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0): keep notices; Apache needs the NOTICE file and change notes. 3. Copyleft (GPL, AGPL) can force releasing your code: get approval before use. 4. No license means no permission to copy. 5. Keep a list of third-party components and licenses. 6. Choose our own license deliberately. 7. Never remove copyright headers.

## legal.ip-trademark
title: Intellectual property and trademarks
roles: risk, ceo, employee
stages: discovery, release
keywords: trademark, ip, intellectual, property, copyright, brand, name, patent
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Search the name in the trademark registry and the web before using it. 2. Avoid names and logos close to famous brands or characters. 3. Contracts must assign work IP to the company. 4. Keep proof of creation (dates, files). 5. Register key marks in the countries you sell. 6. Use only licensed images, fonts and music. 7. Ask a qualified lawyer for filings.

## legal.compliance-check
title: Compliance check for a launch
roles: risk, ceo
stages: release
keywords: compliance, regulation, law, rules, policy, audit, kyc, consumer
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. List the laws for the sector and countries (consumer, data, tax, payments, advertising, accessibility). 2. Map each rule to a product feature or process. 3. Collect evidence that each is met. 4. Note gaps with owner and date. 5. Get specialist review for regulated areas (finance, health, children). 6. Keep records. 7. Re-check when the product or law changes.
