# Project management skills

## pm.project-plan
title: Project plan with stages
roles: pm, ceo, cto
stages: discovery, architecture
keywords: plan, project, stages, milestones, schedule, tasks, deliverables, timeline
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Goal and definition of done in one paragraph. 2. Stages with a deliverable and acceptance check each. 3. Break stages into tasks of 1-3 days with an owner. 4. Mark dependencies and the critical path. 5. Estimate with ranges and add buffer for unknowns. 6. List risks with owner and mitigation. 7. Set a review rhythm and how progress is reported.

## pm.estimation
title: Estimate work
roles: pm, cto, employee
stages: discovery, architecture
keywords: estimate, estimation, effort, time, days, sizing, story, points
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Break work until each piece is small and clear. 2. Estimate each piece as best, likely and worst case. 3. Add integration, testing and review time explicitly. 4. Use past actuals when you have them. 5. Give a range and a confidence, not one number. 6. Re-estimate when scope changes. 7. Track actual vs estimate to improve.

## pm.risk-register
title: Risk register
roles: pm, risk, ceo
stages: discovery, architecture, release
keywords: risk, risks, register, mitigation, issue, contingency, impact, probability
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Write each risk as cause, event, effect. 2. Rate likelihood and impact (1-5). 3. Owner for each risk. 4. Mitigation now and a contingency if it happens. 5. Trigger signs to watch. 6. Review weekly; close or update. 7. Escalate high risks to the decision maker early.

## pm.status-report
title: Status report
roles: pm, ceo, employee
stages: implementation, quality, release
keywords: status, report, update, progress, weekly, stakeholder, summary
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. One line overall: on track / at risk / off track and why. 2. Done since last report. 3. Next steps with owners and dates. 4. Blockers and the decision needed, from whom. 5. Key numbers against plan. 6. Risks that changed. 7. Keep it short; link details instead of pasting them.

## pm.meeting
title: Run a useful meeting
roles: pm, ceo, employee
stages: discovery, architecture, implementation
keywords: meeting, agenda, minutes, decisions, action, items, standup, sync
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Only meet when a decision or discussion needs it. 2. Agenda with goals sent before. 3. Right people only; one facilitator. 4. Start and end on time. 5. Record decisions and action items with owner and date. 6. Share notes within the day. 7. Cancel recurring meetings that stopped being useful.

## pm.retrospective
title: Retrospective after a stage or project
roles: pm, qa, ceo
stages: release, quality
keywords: retrospective, retro, lessons, learned, improve, postmortem, review
source: AGO original (Claude, 2026-11-24)
---
1. Gather facts: what was planned vs what happened, with numbers. 2. What went well and should be kept. 3. What went badly; find causes, not people. 4. Pick at most 3 changes. 5. Give each an owner and a check date. 6. Record lessons where the next project will see them. 7. Follow up that changes happened.
